The Rites
Cast out the bad first. Then call in the good.
The candles come after — flames lit into a room already cleared.
🥚 Opening the Vessel
Officiant"Lena — this egg was prepared for you. Inside it are the tools of this ceremony. Please break it open."
LenaCracks the egg. Items are laid out in the centre of the circle.
Officiant"We begin."
🌿 Sage Smudging
Officiant"We are clearing this home of everything that does not serve Lena."
Circle"We are clearing this home."
Sage CarrierLeads everyone clockwise through each room with the lit bundle.
🧂 Salt Solution Perimeter
Salt BearerLeads the group around the home, sprinkling at doorways and windowsills.
Threshold Voice"Nothing unwelcome crosses this threshold."
Circle"Nothing unwelcome."
🔥 Writing & Burning
OfficiantRound One: "Write what you want gone for Lena and her family. As many slips as you need. Papers are private."
Fire KeeperLights each paper as it is placed in the bowl.
All together"Gone. Done. Over." — after each paper burns.
OfficiantRound Two: "Now write anything any of us would like to leave behind — for yourself, for someone in this circle, for all of us."
All together"Gone. Done. Over."
Officiant"It's done. The room is clear. Now we bring in the light."
🕯️ Three Candles & Spoken Intention
Candle — LenaLights the first candle. "This flame is for Lena."
Candle — FamilyLights the second. "This flame is for Lena, Jim, Maya, and Dom."
Candle — CircleLights the third. "And this one is for all of us."
CircleEach person speaks a wish aloud — or holds it in silence. Both land the same.
🧵 Red Thread
Thread TierWraps and ties the thread around Lena's wrist, seven knots.
CircleOne wish spoken per knot — for Lena, for Jim, for Maya, for Dom, and for the life they are building.
Thread TierOn the last knot: "The protection you wear, you carry home to all of them."
Officiant"Wear it until it falls. When it does, the wishes go free."
🧿 The Evil Eye
Officiant"One last thing. This necklace has already known love. Now it's yours."
Necklace PlacerPlaces the necklace around Lena's neck.
All together"May every eye that wishes you harm look away."
Officiant"We're done. The room is clear, the light is in, and you are held. Let's eat."
